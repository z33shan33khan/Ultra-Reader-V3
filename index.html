# 📚 Ultra Pro Reader A single-file, privacy-first **read-aloud novel reader** that runs entirely in your browser — no install, no server, no account. Import your books (.epub, .pdf, .txt, .md), have them read aloud with highlighting, read translated web novels with gender-aware translation, and enjoy a distraction-free **cinema reading environment**. > One file. `Ultra-Reader-Version4.html`. Open it and start reading. --- ## ✨ Features ### 📖 Reading & Library - **Import almost anything** — `.epub`, `.pdf`, `.txt`, `.md`, `.html` files, pasted text, or chapters straight from a website - **Smart chapter splitting** — detects `Chapter 1`, `第一章`, prologue/epilogue headings, and Chinese numerals - **Chapter tree & page list** — browse chapters with word counts, expand/collapse all - **Library with search & sorting** (title A→Z, recently added, chapter count) - **Rename novels & chapters** inline - **🔖 Bookmarks** — jump straight back to any saved spot - **Progress everything** — book %, chapter %, time-left estimate, and page counter, always visible ### 🔊 Read Aloud (Text-to-Speech) - Sentence-by-sentence narration with **live karaoke-style highlighting** - **Word-level highlighting** as each word is spoken - 100+ narration languages with automatic voice matching - Click any paragraph to start reading from there - **Sentence skip buttons** (⏮/⏭), page skip («/»), stop, and play/pause - **Auto-continue** to the next chapter when one finishes - **Keeps reading reliably on mobile** — wake-lock keeps the screen on, and a watchdog restarts speech if the phone silences it in the background (no more stalling mid-chapter) - **Pronunciation rules** — teach it names ("Qi → Chi", "Xiao → Shiao"); combined names are auto-split into syllables. Audio only, text untouched - **Skip lists** — automatically skip headings, author notes, etc. - Adjustable speed, voice, pitch and more ### 🀄 Smart Translation with Gender Awareness - Built-in translation for foreign-language chapters (Google engine, multiple styles) - **Smart Chinese gender fix**: Chinese writes 他/她 the same way, so translators call every character "he". The reader detects each character's gender from 她, titles like 小姐/夫人, and name cues — then uses **she/her for women and he/him for men** in the English translation - Add your own character corrections (name → gender) ### 🎬 Reading Environment (Cinema Mode) Three focus modes — pick your favourite, with an **Auto with play** option that turns the lights off the moment you press play: - **🕯 Lights off** — everything except your book and the HUD fades into darkness - **⛶ Full screen** — true full-screen reading - **⧉ Pop-out** — opens the reader in a **real, full-size, freely resizable window** that carries your exact book, position and lights-off state (falls back to an always-on-top mini window with its own control dock) - **✕ Lights on** — instantly back ### 🧭 The Orb (floating menu) While reading, the control bar folds into a small golden sphere that **docks to the left or right screen edge** — away from your text: - **Tap** → instant **play/pause** - **Long-press (~½ second)** → fans out a labelled menu bar (Contents · Prev page · Bookmark · Prev line · Next line · Stop · Voice · Next page) - **Drag** → reposition along the edge or fling it to the other side (remembered) ### 📌 Always-on Reading HUD A slim strip that **never disappears in any mode**: `Book title · Chapter name · Time remaining · Page X / Y` plus a second strip with live **BOOK %** and **CHAPTER %** progress bars. Present in lights-off, full screen and the pop-out window alike. ### 🌐 Website Reader (mini browser) - Read serialised novels straight from the web with **Reader view** (clean text) or **Live view** (for signing in to sites) - Tabbed browsing with a speed dial, history, chapter ‹ › navigation - Auto-continue through web chapters with narration ### 💾 Storage & Privacy - **100% local** — books and settings never leave your device - **Auto-saved forever**: book text is stored in **IndexedDB** (hundreds of MB of space), so novels survive closing the site — no re-uploading the same document every visit - **💾 Backup / 📥 Restore** — export your whole library (books + bookmarks + progress) to a single `.json` file, keep it on your PC or a USB drive, and restore it anywhere (great for moving devices or as a safety net) ### 🎨 Comfort & Appearance - Dark & light themes, custom reader fonts (including installed system fonts), adjustable text size - Clean typography (Literata) and a warm parchment design --- ## 🚀 Getting Started 1. **Host it or open it** - Easiest: push `Ultra-Reader-Version4.html` to a GitHub repo and enable **GitHub Pages** — then open the URL on any device (you can also "Add to Home Screen" on a phone for an app-like feel) - Or simply double-click the file to open it locally 2. **Add a book**: 📂 open a file, 📋 paste text, or 🌐 load from a website 3. **Press ▶** — the app folds into the edge-docked orb, lights go off (if Auto with play is on), and your book is read aloud > Tip: the transport bar stays at the bottom centre when paused, and folds into the edge orb while reading. --- ## ⌨️ Controls Cheat-Sheet | Action | How | |---|---| | Play / pause | `Space`, tap the orb, or the ▶ button | | Stop | ■ button (also turns the lights back on) | | Previous / next sentence | ⏮ / ⏭ or the orb menu | | Previous / next page | « / » | | Contents & page list | ☰ (opens the side drawer) | | Voice & reading settings | ⚙ | | Bookmark this spot | 🔖 | | Lights off / Full screen / Pop-out | Buttons above the book, or in the floating bar | | Turn the lights back on | ✕ Lights on (floating bar) or Stop | --- ## 🧰 Tech Notes - **Single self-contained HTML file** — no build step, no dependencies except the pdf.js CDN (loaded only for PDF imports) - **Storage:** IndexedDB for book text (large quota), localStorage for metadata, progress, bookmarks and settings - **Speech:** the Web Speech API (`speechSynthesis`) — works best in Chrome/Edge - **Pop-out:** uses the Document Picture-in-Picture API on Chromium desktop browsers; other browsers gracefully fall back to lights-off reading - **Privacy:** there is no backend, no analytics, no account. Everything stays in your browser ## ✅ Browser Support | Browser | Reading | Pop-out window | Notes | |---|---|---|---| | Chrome / Edge (desktop & Android) | ✅ Best | ✅ | Recommended | | Firefox | ✅ | ⛔ (falls back to Lights off) | | | Safari / iOS | ⚠️ Partial | ⛔ | Voices vary; no Document PiP | --- ## ❓ FAQ **Do I have to re-upload my book every time I open the site?** No. Books are saved automatically and restored when you return — including your exact chapter and page. **Can I move my library to another device?** Yes — **Library → 💾 Backup** on the first device, then **📥 Restore** on the other. **Why does the voice stop on my phone sometimes?** Mobile browsers aggressively silence background audio. The app fights this with a screen wake-lock and an auto-restart watchdog, but if your browser kills the tab, reopening it resumes at your saved position. **Is my data uploaded anywhere?** No. Everything lives in your browser's storage on your device. --- *Ultra Pro Reader — your books, read aloud, in a beautiful light.* 📖🎧
